## Key Insights from the Analysis:
Correlation Results:
- LIBRA Volume vs. PumpFun Volume → Correlation: 0.978 (Strong positive correlation)
- LIBRA Volume vs. Hyperliquid Revenue → Correlation: 0.986 (Very strong positive correlation) 

This suggests that as LIBRA trading volume increases, both PumpFun volume and Hyperliquid revenue tend to rise significantly.

- The $LIBRA scandal triggered a surge in trading activity across multiple platforms.
